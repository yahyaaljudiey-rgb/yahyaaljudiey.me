Book: “Marib Memoirs”

The story of three martyrs from my family — Mazen, Nasim and Ramzi — written and designed by me.

  1. Part one: 94 pages
  2. Prologue and 19 chapters
  3. A5 format

1The idea

A narrative book recording the lives of three young men from my family who fell in Marib: Mazen, then Nasim, then Ramzi, each with his own part opened by a card introducing him. Part one, on Mazen, is complete: from the fronts of al-Jubail, al-Bayadh and Mount Qurud, through his wounds and return to the trenches, to his last letters.

2What I made

Author, editor, designer and typesetter.

I designed the whole book: the cover of Marib’s mountains at dawn, the dedication, part openers, letter cards and photographs placed at their moments — keeping the source texts exactly as written.

  • Prologue and 19 chapters
  • A5 format
