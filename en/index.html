Research & Publications

On comparative religion, ethics and thought, free to read and download.

  1. Leadership Ethics and Shifts in Collective Compliance in Judaism and Islam: A Comparative Study

    A comparative study tracing the criterion of leadership ethics in the Israelite experience between Moses and Aaron, set against shifts in Arab compliance during the Prophet’s ﷺ lifetime and after his death. It focuses on the leader’s influence, the structure of the community, and the standards of legitimacy and obedience.

  2. The Ethical Criterion between Intention and Outcome: An Analytical Study (Islamic Perspective)

    An analytical study examining ethical judgement between intention and consequences, with a focused discussion through the Islamic ethical framework. It clarifies how purpose, accountability, and outcomes interact in moral evaluation.

  3. The Relationship between Faith and Ethical Conduct in Judaism, Christianity, and Islam

    A study approaching the relationship between belief and ethical action across the three faiths: how does faith produce conduct, and where does the tension between text and practice lie? It offers a comparative framework for the sources of moral obligation and the standards of evaluation.
