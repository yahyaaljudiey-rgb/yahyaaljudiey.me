Part one

Work I made myself

Three of many: each began with a real problem, what I built for it, and what came of it.

  1. 1Apps & platforms

    Yemeni Laws

    A complete legal library that works offline, with an AI adviser that answers from the statutes themselves.

    The problem
    An Android and web app that puts Yemeni legislation in the pocket of lawyers, students and citizens: unified search across the statutes, plus practical tools that save hours — an inheritance calculator, ready legal templates, and a guide to courts and procedure.
    What I did
    Concept, experience design, build, the semantic search engine, and releases.
    Outcome
    413 laws indexed · Fully offline · Semantic search with article citations
    Full story →

  2. 2Books & print

    Book: “Laws of Conflict and State-Building”

    A reading of the Prophetic Seerah — written, typeset and designed end to end, in print and digital editions.

    The problem
    A book that reads the Prophetic Seerah through the patterns that govern conflict and the rise of states. I wrote it and produced it myself — from the cover and chapter art to the typesetting and footnotes.
    What I did
    Author, designer, and typesetter.
    Outcome
    102 pages · A5 · 6 chapters · Print and digital editions
    Full story →

  3. 3Apps & platforms

    Al-Hikmah Arabic Department Platform

    A system that runs an entire teaching department: four portals, Arabic PDF reports, and public certificate verification.

    The problem
    I built the platform while heading the department, to bring together what had been scattered across spreadsheets and paper: attendance, exams, monthly reports and certificates. Supervisor, teacher, head and student each have their own portal.
    What I did
    Analysis, design, build, and operation.
    Outcome
    4 role-based portals · Bilingual · Public certificate verification

Research & Publications

  1. Leadership Ethics and Shifts in Collective Compliance in Judaism and Islam: A Comparative Study

    A comparative study tracing the criterion of leadership ethics in the Israelite experience between Moses and Aaron, set against shifts in Arab compliance during the Prophet’s ﷺ lifetime and after his death. It focuses on the leader’s influence, the structure of the community, and the standards of legitimacy and obedience.

Part three

The path so far

An educator specialising in Qur’anic instruction, Islamic studies, and the Arabic language, with more than twelve years of experience across Qur’anic centres and schools. I combine scholarly grounding with the ability to make material accessible, and I care about cultivating Islamic values and conduct in students.

I currently serve as Head of the Arabic Department at Al-Hikmah Academy in the United Kingdom, while reading for a PhD in Philosophy and Comparative Religion at the International Islamic University Malaysia, following an MA in Usul al-Din and Comparative Religion with distinction. My research bridges comparative religious studies and contemporary political thought.
