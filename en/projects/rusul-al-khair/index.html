Programmes·In progress·The Youth Club

Rusul al-Khair Programme

A two-stage youth programme, with a tracking system where teachers log every session for every student.

  • 2 stages · 4 pillars
  • Paperless
  • System-proposed progression, supervisor-approved

About the work

I designed the programme from the ground up: a foundation stage then a formation stage, built on four constant pillars — faith and values, knowledge, skills, and community service — each stage with its own curriculum, goals, and a graduation for those who meet its criteria.

It runs on a paperless system: teachers log attendance and lessons with a tap, the system proposes who is ready to move up, and the supervisor approves.

Programme, curriculum, and system design.
